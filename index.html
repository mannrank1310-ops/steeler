Precision Workflow

Manufacturing Process

From raw iron ore to finished industrial-grade steel products, our advanced production pipeline guarantees superior metallurgical integrity.

01

Raw Material

Carefully sourced high-grade iron ore, scrap metal, and high-purity carbon inputs tested for baseline purity before smelting.

02

Melting & Processing

Electric arc furnaces melt raw inputs at extreme temperatures with precise alloy additions to achieve target chemical composition.

03

Rolling / Forming

Molten steel is cast and passed through high-pressure rolling mills to shape structural beams, plates, coils, and bars.

04

Quality Inspection

Rigorous non-destructive testing, ultrasonic flaw detection, and tensile strength checks are performed on batch samples.

05

Finishing

Surface treatment, anti-corrosion coating, thermal treatments, and precision cutting to exact customer dimensions.

06

Packaging & Delivery

Weather-resistant packaging and secure logistics management ensuring safe transit to domestic and international project sites.
